# Tapo P110M Smart WiFi Plug

These are nice and small, and support up to 15 Amps.  At the moment, we've been unable to interface with the energy monitor chip, but otherwise have full control of the device.  We're continuing to try and figure out the energy monitoring, and will update this repo as soon as we've figured it out.

We've worked with v1.6 plugs, which use an RTL8720CM controller.  The energy monitor chip is BL0937.
